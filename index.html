Privacy Policy for Sultan Tracking

Effective Date: November 2026

Sultan Tracking is a browser extension designed to help users monitor their daily website usage, set browsing limits, and stay focused.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Local Storage Data: By default, all browsing durations, daily limits, and focus schedules are stored locally on your device using browser storage.
  • Optional Cloud Sync Data: If you choose to sign in using your email or Google account, we collect your email address, authentication tokens, and aggregated domain usage statistics to synchronize your data across your devices via Supabase.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to provide the core functionality of the extension: tracking active tab domains, enforcing user-defined limits/focus blocks, and syncing your personal statistics across your own devices.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal data or browsing history to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Cloud-synced data is protected with Row-Level Security (RLS) so only your authenticated account can access your records. You can delete your cloud data at any time from the extension settings.
